# Hallway test: 3 to 5 strangers, 10 minutes each

The one test only people can run. Find 3 to 5 people who have never seen Icarus (other teams, visitors, staff). Use
the hosted link on **their own phone or a laptop**. Don't explain anything; that's the point.

## What to say (read it out, word for word)

> "This is a finance app. Please buy 1,000 baht of gold. Then I'll ask you to do it again a different way. Think out
> loud while you go: say what you're looking for and what's confusing."

## The tasks

1. **Race 1, by hand.** On a laptop: in the side panel press *Race 1: without Icarus* → Start, then buy 1,000 baht of
   gold by tapping through the app. The stopwatch stops at *Confirm purchase*. (On a phone: start a timer yourself.)
2. **Race 2, with Icarus.** Press *Race 2: with Icarus* → Start, and say "Put 1,000 baht in gold" (or press *No mic?
   Let Icarus hear it*). Tap *Confirm purchase*.
3. **Ask anything.** "Ask Icarus one question you'd really have about this app." Don't suggest one.
4. **One minute free.** Let them play. Watch where they go.

## Afterwards

- Send the table to the person fixing things. Fix whatever two or more people tripped on, then run one more person.
- The race times are the proof for the pitch: report the median of race 1 and race 2, not the best run.
