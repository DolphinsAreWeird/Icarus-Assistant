# Knowledge provenance

`records.json` contains short paraphrased source-backed records plus an explicitly synthetic app-rule record. These are a starter corpus, not a complete crypto encyclopedia or an expert-approved advisory policy.

English and Thai records share the same source identity. The Thai wording is a draft, not a quotation or an independently reviewed translation. Check financial meaning with a Thai speaker. Source checking is not expert suitability review.

Retrieve with explicit aliases and topics. Adding a new record requires: a source appropriate to the claim, preservation of conditions/units, a section reference, review status, and bilingual retrieval cases. A page's marketing statements do not establish unrestricted redemption rights or independent safety assessments.

No live market price belongs in these records. Prices/history/calculations come from typed tools. The saved Binance history is real historical USDT data; user profiles and app fees are fictional. There is no verified U-topia fee schedule or connected custody/execution service.

Check source changes before the presentation. Static source-backed text can become outdated, and this starter corpus has no automatic approval of external changes.
